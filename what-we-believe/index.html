What We Believe

Our Statement of Faith

Our beliefs are centered on Scripture and centered on the person and work of Jesus Christ. They shape how we understand God, the Bible, humanity, salvation, and God's redemptive plan for the world. These core convictions guide everything we do from how we worship and serve to how we love our community and make disciples. We invite you to explore what we believe and discover the hope found in a relationship with Jesus Christ.

What We Believe

Our beliefs guide and inspire our mission to help people find and follow Jesus. The Journey Church is affiliated with the Assemblies of God, and our beliefs are rooted in Scripture and aligned with the Assemblies of God's Statement of Fundamental Truths. Here's what we believe.

The Scriptures Inspired

We believe the Bible is God's inspired Word and our final authority for truth, faith, and daily living. It reveals who God is and guides every next step we take.

2 Timothy 3:16-17  ·  2 Peter 1:20-21

The One True God

We believe there is one eternal God who exists in three persons, Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, perfectly united and fully God.

Deuteronomy 6:4  ·  Matthew 28:19

The Deity of the Lord Jesus Christ

We believe jesus is fully God and fully man. He came to earth, lived without sin, died for us, rose again, and made a way for us to know God.

John 1:1-14  ·  Colossians 2:9

The Fall of Man

We believe humanity was created good but chose sin, which separated us from God and created our need for a Savior.

Genesis 3:1-7  ·  Romans 5:12

The Salvation of Man

We believe salvation is found through faith in Jesus alone. Through His grace we are forgiven, made new, and restored to relationship with God.

Ephesians 2:8-9  ·  Romans 10:9-10

The Ordinances of the Church

We believe water baptism and communion are important practices Jesus gave the Church to publicly express faith and remember His sacrifice.

Matthew 28:19  ·  1 Corinthians 11:23-26

The Baptism in the Holy Spirit

We believe believers can experience a special empowering of the Holy Spirit to help them live boldly and fulfill God's mission.

Acts 1:8  ·  Acts 2:1-4

The Initial Physical Evidence of the Baptism in the Holy Spirit

We believe speaking in tongues is the initial outward evidence of receiving the baptism in the Holy Spirit as seen in Scripture.

Acts 2:4  ·  Acts 10:44-46

Sanctification

We believe following Jesus is a lifelong journey of becoming more like Him as the Holy Spirit transforms our hearts and lives.

Romans 12:1-2  ·  1 Peter 1:15-16
