Marker 01  /  Why give

Thank you for considering a gift to The Journey Church. Every contribution helps us fulfill our mission of helping people find and follow Jesus.

As a new church plant, your generosity creates opportunities for people in our community to hear the Gospel, build meaningful relationships, and take their next step in faith. We believe giving is an act of worship and a tangible way to participate in God's work. Whether your gift is large or small, every dollar makes an eternal impact.
