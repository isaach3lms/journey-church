Six ways to build this church

Our launch team is made up of individuals and families using their gifts to help create a welcoming, life-giving church where people can encounter Jesus and find community.

You do not need special experience. You need a willing heart and a desire to make a difference. If you are ready to help build something bigger than yourself and make an eternal impact from day one, we would love to connect with you.
