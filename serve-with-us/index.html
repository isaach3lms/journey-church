Be Part Of The Journey

The Journey Church is being built by people who believe God is doing something special in our community, and we'd love for you to be part of it. Our Launch Team is made up of individuals and families who are using their gifts to help create a welcoming, life-giving church where people can encounter Jesus and find community. Whether you're passionate about hospitality, kids ministry, worship, production, outreach, prayer, or behind-the-scenes support, there's a place for you to serve. You don't need special experience, just a willing heart and a desire to make a difference.
