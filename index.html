Marker 01  /  A church for the journey

Following the Spirit, not standing still

The Journey Church exists to help people live adventurously expectant as they take their next step with God. Inspired by Romans 8:15 in The Message, we believe the Christian life is not about standing still. It is about following the Spirit with the confidence of sons and daughters who know they are loved by their Father.

Every day is an invitation to trust God more deeply, embrace what He is doing, and move forward in faith. No matter where you are on your journey, we want to help you discover what it looks like to live with open hands, a willing heart, and a constant expectation that God is not finished writing your story.

Marker 02  /  Why another church in Jackson

The people of Jackson love Jackson

This is a community built on strong families, deep relationships, and genuine pride in the place they call home. From youth sports and school events to neighborhood gatherings and local businesses, life here is shaped by connection and a commitment to the next generation.

As Jackson grows, so does the opportunity to help people find something even greater than a place to belong. Many have a faith background, and still there are countless people searching for authentic community, meaningful relationships, and a deeper connection with God. We believe The Journey Church is positioned to meet that need.

Marker 03  /  Meet our pastors

Brandon & Whitney Tanksley

Brandon and Whitney are the lead pastors of The Journey Church, a church built on the belief that life with God is a journey meant to be lived adventurously expectant.

Brandon serves as a chaplain with Crown Hospice, walking closely with individuals and families through some of life's most meaningful and difficult moments. Whitney is a 4th grade teacher at South Elementary in Jackson, where she pours into the next generation every day. Together they are raising three kids, Camdon, Tucker, and Aubrey.
