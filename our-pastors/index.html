Our leadership

Brandon & Whitney Tanksley

Back home, building something meaningful in the community they grew up around.

Marker 01  /  The family

Brandon and Whitney Tanksley are the founders of The Journey Church in Jackson, Missouri, a church built on the belief that life with God is a journey meant to be lived adventurously expectant.

Brandon serves as a chaplain with Crown Hospice, walking closely with individuals and families through some of life's most meaningful and difficult moments. Whitney is a 4th grade teacher at South Elementary in Jackson, where she pours into the next generation on a daily basis.

Together they are raising three kids, Camdon, Tucker, and Aubrey, who keep them busy and deeply connected to the Jackson community. Their boys are active in sports across the area, and as a family they love spending time outdoors and embracing every adventure they can find.

Marker 02  /  A decade of ministry

Over the past decade, Brandon and Whitney have served in nearly every area of ministry: kids, youth, young adults, and even helping plant and lead a church campus.

Ministry has taken them many places, but nothing compares to being back home, serving and building something meaningful in the community they grew up around. They are proud Cape Central graduates. Do not hold that against them. God has clearly planted and grown their heart for Jackson.

Marker 03  /  The vision

People moving forward, not standing still

Their passion for this community is unmatched, and their vision for The Journey Church is simple: to see people live adventurously expectant as they take their next step in their journey with God.

They believe the church should meet people where they are, walk with them authentically, and help them move forward in their faith. At the core of it all is a deep conviction that following Jesus is not meant to be stagnant. It is meant to be lived boldly, faithfully, and adventurously expectant.
